## Method
- Sample: the 68 early-stage companies on the AIAgentSearch.ai AI agent candidate list that our editors reviewed in September 2026, sourced mainly from accelerator rosters, funding coverage and category round-ups. Not a random sample; results describe the companies reviewed.
- Reading: each company's own homepage (and product page where needed) was read on October 31, 2026, with AI-assisted tooling; the wording and address were recorded. Classification rules and borderline calls were decided by the Publisher.
- Test: AIAgentSearch.ai Editorial Standards, Section 7: an AI agent "perceives, decides, and acts autonomously toward a defined goal". Approval of a final outcome passes; approval of every action does not.
- Agent washing: counted only where a company's own website describes its own product as an AI agent and the product does not meet the test.
- What it measures: public claims judged against the test. Products were not tested.

## Codebook
| measure | meaning |
|---|---|
| reviewed | companies in the sample |
| left_out_changed_or_unconfirmed | product changed since September, status conflicting, product not shown, or official site not identified |
| classified | reviewed minus left out |
| meets_agent_test | takes goal-directed action on its own |
| assistive | a person starts or approves each action |
| not_agent_product_or_mainly_human_service | tools or infrastructure for other agents, analysis or messaging software, research services, or a service delivered mainly by people |
| did_not_meet_test | assistive + not_agent_product_or_mainly_human_service |
| own_site_calls_product_ai_agent | the company's own website describes its product as an AI agent |
| own_site_calls_product_ai_agent_and_meets_test | of those, meets the test |
| own_site_calls_product_ai_agent_and_does_not_meet_test | of those, does not (the agent-washing count) |
| meets_test_but_own_site_does_not_call_it_an_agent | meets the test but its homepage does not use the word |
